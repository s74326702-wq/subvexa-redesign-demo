What to check before buying a gift card

5 Nov 2026 · 4 min read · Buyer guide

500+ gift cards · 50,000+ happy customers · 4.2/5 on Trustpilot
🔒 128-bit SSL secure🛡️ PCI DSS compliant💬 WhatsApp & Telegram support✉️ support@subvexa.shop

Two minutes of checking before you pay saves the most common gift-card headaches. Work through this list every time.

1 · Region — the big one

Buy the region that matches your account. A USA code only works on a USA account. Check the flag badge on the product card.

2 · Denomination & currency

Check the face value and the currency you pay in. Compare the final total at checkout, not just the face value — fees and conversion change the real cost.

3 · Expiry

Some regional cards carry an expiry date. Read the redemption-requirements box on the product page before paying.

4 · Redemption method

Code, account login, or direct top-up? Know what you will receive and where it appears — on Subvexa, codes appear in My Orders and in your email.

5 · Buyer protection

Check the seller's protection: activation guarantee, unviewed-code refund, and what happens if delivery fails. On Subvexa, Buyer Protection is shown on every product page.

6 · Delivery email

Make sure the email on your account is correct — the delivery email goes there. Check spam/promotions if it does not arrive.

7 · If a code fails, don't buy twice

Keep the order confirmation and the exact error message, and contact support with your order number. A second purchase will not fix the first one — and delivered codes cannot be refunded.
