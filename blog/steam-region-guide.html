Steam region guide: buy the right card first

5 Nov 2026 · 4 min read · Buyer guide

🔒 128-bit SSL secure🛡️ PCI DSS compliant💬 WhatsApp & Telegram support✉️ support@subvexa.shop

The single most common Steam mistake is buying the wrong region. A USA Steam code only works on a USA Steam account. A KSA code only works on a KSA account. The region is printed on the card — check it before you pay, not after.

1 · Match the region to your account

Open Steam, check your account country, then buy the same region: USA, Europe, KSA, UAE, India, Turkey, Hong Kong or Singapore. On Subvexa each region is its own card with a flag badge, so you can see it at a glance.

2 · Compare the final cost, not just the face value

Compare the total at checkout — face value plus any fee — in the currency you actually pay. A cheaper-looking card in another currency can cost more after conversion.

3 · Check expiry and redemption rules

Some regional cards carry an expiry date. Read the redemption-requirements box on the product page before paying.

4 · If a code fails, do not buy twice

Keep your order confirmation and the exact error message, and contact support@subvexa.shop with your order number. Buying a second card will not fix the first one — and delivered codes cannot be refunded.
