What happens after you pay?

  1. Validating payment — we confirm your USDT payment on the blockchain.
  2. Cards are being issued — the supplier issues your code or account.
  3. Show Code — your code appears in My Orders and in your email. Copy and redeem.

If delivery fails after payment, you are refunded — see the Return Policy.

Common questions

Do I need an account to buy?

Yes — orders are tied to your account so your codes are always in My Orders, even if the email is lost.

How long does delivery take?

Usually under a minute after payment is confirmed. If an order is still not delivered after 30 minutes, contact support@subvexa.shop with your order number.

What if I pay the wrong amount?

Pay the exact amount shown at checkout. If you over- or under-pay, contact support with the transaction hash — do not pay twice.
