📦 Orders & delivery

Where is my code after I pay?

In My Orders — click Show Code — and in your email. Both appear the moment the order is DELIVERED, usually ~30 seconds after payment is confirmed.

How long does delivery take?

Usually under a minute after payment is confirmed. If an order is still not delivered after 30 minutes, contact support with your order number.

I paid but nothing was delivered. What now?

Check My Orders first — the order may still be validating. If it is still not delivered after 30 minutes, contact support@subvexa.shop with your order number and transaction hash. If delivery fails, you are refunded — see the Return Policy.

Can I resend my code?

Yes — open the order in My Orders and use the Resend code button. The delivery email is re-sent to your account email.

💳 Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?

USDT (BEP20) is live. See the full Payment Methods page.

What if I pay the wrong amount?

Pay the exact amount shown at checkout. If you over- or under-pay, contact support with the transaction hash — do not pay twice.

Why do I have to tick the consent box at checkout?

For digital goods, EU rules require your express consent to instant delivery and your acknowledgement that the withdrawal right ends once the code is revealed. The box is not pre-ticked — you choose.

🌍 Regions & redemption

I bought the wrong region. Can I exchange it?

Delivered codes cannot be exchanged. Check the region on the product page before paying — see the Steam region guide.

How do I redeem a Steam code?

Steam → Games → Redeem a Steam Wallet Code, paste and confirm. Full steps: How to redeem a Steam code.

My code shows as invalid or already used.

Contact support with your order number and the exact error message. Do not publish the code anywhere.

↩️ Refunds

Can I get a refund?

If you paid but no code was delivered, yes — contact support. After a valid code is delivered, change-of-mind returns are not accepted. Faulty, wrong or undelivered products are replaced or refunded. See the Return Policy.

What is Buyer Protection?

Every order is covered: activation guarantee (replace or refund if a code won't activate), unviewed-code refund, and an escrow-style hold — payment is held until delivery is confirmed.

👤 Account

Do I need an account to buy?

Yes — orders are tied to your account so your codes are always in My Orders, even if the email is lost. You need to be 18+.

How do I change my email or password?

Go to My Account and edit your profile (demo — saved only in this browser).

What are Subvexa Rewards points?

You earn 5% back in demo points on every delivered order. 100 points = $1 off your next order (demo). Your balance is on the homepage and in My Account.
